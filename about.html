About Prime Time

Prime Time Miami is a Miami based dealer sourcing the finest watches available from brands such as Rolex, Audemars Piguet, Patek Philippe and Richard Mille.

Every piece is sourced through verified partners and independently authenticated by trusted watch specialists before it reaches you.

Real watches, for true collectors.

The references and years we can source change constantly. If you do not see what you are looking for, submit a sourcing request or send us a DM and we will get back to you as soon as possible.

Prime Time Miami is not a storefront. It is a concierge service built on trust, discretion and access. Whether you are buying, selling or trading, we approach each transaction as if it were our own.
