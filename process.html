Our Process

Consultation

Tell us what you're looking for: brand, reference, condition, budget. We listen, advise, and set realistic expectations. References and years available change constantly, so we'll confirm exactly what we can source.

Sourcing

We activate a global network of trusted dealers, collectors, and private sellers. Leads are vetted for provenance and history.

Authentication

Pieces are independently authenticated by trusted watch specialists. Dial, movement, case, and bracelet are verified.

Delivery

Fully insured and shipped via secure courier with detailed documentation, photography, and tracking.
